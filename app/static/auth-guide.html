Authentication guide

How to obtain your CIS WorkBench cookies.txt

← Back to the app

Why is this needed?

CIS WorkBench requires you to sign in before downloading benchmarks. The tool does not store your username or password: instead it reuses the session you already opened in your browser, represented by session cookies.

A session cookie is a temporary "pass" that says "this user is already authenticated". It is not your password and it expires over time. When it does, just repeat these steps.

Step by step — Chrome / Edge / Brave

  1. Install a cookie-export extension. Recommended: "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" (Chrome Web Store). It is open-source and runs locally — it does not send your cookies anywhere.
  2. Sign in to CIS WorkBench: open workbench.cisecurity.org and log in with your account.
  3. Export: with that tab open and signed in, click the extension icon (top-right) and press "Export". Format: Netscape.
  4. Save the downloaded cookies.txt file.

Firefox

Same as above, but install the "cookies.txt" extension (by Lennon Hill). Open WorkBench while signed in → click the extension → export.

Upload the file

  1. Back in the app, under "1 · CIS WorkBench authentication", choose your cookies.txt (Option B).
  2. Press "Sign in". The auth badge turns green.
🔒 The app does not keep the uploaded file: it deletes it after the session is validated. Treat your cookies.txt as a credential and don't share it.
